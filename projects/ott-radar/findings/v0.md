# v0 — baseline tells

Route `/v0`. Built from the naive prompt with no constitution, no references, no
screenshot review, no design skills. Commit `7662247`.

Evidence: `lab/shots/v0/` (390 / 768 / 1440 × light / dark).

This list is the scoring rubric. Every later drill is marked against it:
**fixed / unchanged / newly introduced**.

---

## What v0 got right

Recording this first, because the honest baseline is better than the stereotype and
pretending otherwise would inflate every later drill's apparent gain.

- **Dark mode is implemented** — 25 `dark:` variants, and the captures genuinely differ.
  Not inherited, actually written.
- **Date-grouped information architecture** — grouping by release day is the correct
  structure for this content. Not an obvious choice, and it got there unprompted.
- **Sport date ranges render correctly** — "Aug 15 – Aug 21" rather than a single date.
- **Missing artwork does not crash** — all six null-poster releases render something.
- **Several banned defaults avoided without being told**: no purple gradient, no centered
  hero with two buttons, no emoji, no gradient text.

Implication: the `v0 → v1` delta will be smaller than the spec predicted, because the
constitution's ban list was already mostly satisfied. That is a finding about the
constitution's value, not a failure of the drill.

## Tells

### Structural

**T1 — Fixed-column grid marooning single-item rows.**
Aug 12, Aug 17, and Aug 21 each contain one release. Each renders as a lone card at far
left with roughly 80% of the row empty. Three of six date groups look broken. The grid
was chosen once and applied to groups of wildly varying length.

**T2 — Page content hugs the left edge.**
`max-w-6xl` with left-aligned groups leaves ~40% of a 1440 viewport permanently unused.

**T3 — Uniform card treatment regardless of content type.**
A cricket fixture spanning a week, a Tamil movie, and a returning prestige series all get
an identical card. Nothing on the page is more important than anything else.

**T4 — Card heights vary within a row.**
"Bharat Bhagya Vidhaata" wraps to two lines and pushes its metadata down, breaking
baseline alignment with the cards beside it.

### The missing-artwork state — the worst failure

**T5 — Absent posters render as a saturated colour block with two initials.**
"BB", "1B", "IT", "ID", "PP" in white on flat platform-brand colour. It reads as an error
state, not a decision.

**T6 — The initials are meaningless.**
"1B" for *108 Base Hospital: Uri*. "IT" for *India Tour of Sri Lanka*.

**T7 — Two different shows both render "BB" on the same screen.**
*Bharat Bhagya Vidhaata* (purple) and *Bigg Boss Agnipariksha* (blue), one row apart.
The fallback actively creates ambiguity where the real data had none.

**T8 — Fallback blocks are the most visually dominant objects on the page.**
Flat saturated colour outweighs every real poster. The six items with the *least*
information shout the loudest.

### Colour and identity

**T9 — The page has no colour identity of its own.**
Neutral greys plus whichever borrowed platform brand colours happen to be in view (up to
seven of the eleven defined, on the India render). Netflix red, ZEE5 purple, and SonyLIV red
become the de facto accent by default. This is the deepest tell on the list: not "the
colours are wrong" but "no colour decision was ever made."

### Typography and hierarchy

**T10 — The type scale is barren.**
`text-xs` and `text-sm` account for 8 of 12 size usages; one `text-3xl` headline, one
`text-2xl`. No mid-tier, so nothing can carry intermediate emphasis.

**T11 — Metadata is an undifferentiated dot-separated string.**
"Series · English · Aug 12" — identical weight, size, and colour for three different
kinds of fact. Nothing is scannable.

**T12 — The date is printed twice on every card.**
Group header says "WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 12"; the card beneath repeats "Aug 12".

### Component reflexes

**T13 — Platform badge is a pill with a coloured dot.**
The dot encodes nothing the adjacent label does not already say.

**T14 — Elevation and radius are ad hoc.**
`shadow-lg` once, `shadow-sm` twice, `rounded-2xl` twice, `rounded-full` five times. No
rule — each value chosen locally.

**T15 — The region toggle is the loudest element after the title.**
A high-contrast segmented pill, top-right, for a control most visitors will touch once.

**T16 — "New today" badge appears on exactly one date group.**
On a page whose entire subject is one week, "today" is unanchored, and applying it to
Aug 16 alone reads as a bug.

**T17 — Footer disclaimer is low-contrast grey on grey**, near-illegible at ~11px.
